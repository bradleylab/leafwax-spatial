## SUMMARY STATISTICS

### Global Calibrations - n-C29 Alkane

- **Slope range**: 0.548 to 0.92
- **Intercept range**: -148 to -117
- **R² range**: 0.71 to 0.80
- **Sample size**: 177 to 750

### Regional Variability

- **Driest regions** (Basin & Range, <400mm): Slope = 1.69, R² = 0.84
- **Wetter regions** (Great Plains, >400mm): Slope = 0.67, R² = 0.64
- **Tropical forests** (Andes-Amazon): Slope = 0.89, R² = 0.97 (plot means)
- **Hyperarid regions** (Chile): Departure from global relationship observed

### Key Findings

1. Global relationships are robust (R² = 0.71-0.80)
2. Slopes vary regionally: 0.55 to 1.69
3. Aridity strongly affects fractionation in some regions
4. Life-form differences are significant
5. n-C29 alkane most commonly calibrated
6. n-C28 acid shows similar global correlations to n-C29 alkane
