## SUMMARY STATISTICS

### Global Calibrations - n-C29 Alkane

- **Slope range**: 0.548 to 0.92
- **Intercept range**: -156 to -117
- **R² range**: 0.66 to 0.81
- **Sample size**: 177 to 750

### Regional Variability

- **Driest regions** (Basin & Range, <400mm): Slope = 1.69, R² = 0.84
- **Wetter regions** (Great Plains, >400mm): Slope = 0.67, R² = 0.64
- **Tropical forests** (Andes-Amazon): Slope = 0.89, R² = 0.97 (plot means)
- **Tibetan Plateau aquatic** (C23 alkane): Slope = 1.89, R² = 0.94
- **Tibetan Plateau terrestrial** (C29 alkane): Slope = 0.70, R² = 0.66
- **Hyperarid regions** (Chile): Departure from global relationship observed

### Key Findings

1. **Global relationships** are robust (R² = 0.71-0.81)
2. **Slopes vary dramatically by region**: 0.42 to 1.95
3. **Aquatic vs terrestrial** n-alkanes show different slopes (Tibet: 1.89 vs 0.70)
4. **Aridity** strongly affects fractionation in some regions
5. **Life-form differences** are significant (0.52 to 1.21)
6. **n-C29 alkane** most commonly calibrated
7. **n-C28 acid** shows similar global correlations to n-C29 alkane
8. **Water source matters**: Lake water vs precipitation vs surface water show different relationships
